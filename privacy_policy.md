# Privacy Policy for All You Need a Video

**Last updated: August 3, 2025**

## Introduction
All You Need a Video ("we", "us", or "our") is a mobile application designed to provide video-related functionalities. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we handle your data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you use our app.

This policy applies to the All You Need a Video app and related services. If our app integrates with third-party services (e.g., analytics or advertising platforms), their privacy practices are governed by their respective policies, and we encourage you to review them.

## Information We Collect
We collect the following types of data to provide and improve our services:

### Personal Information
- **Email Address**: Collected if you create an account or contact us for support (optional).
- **User-Generated Content**: Videos or other content you upload or create within the app.

### Usage Data
- **Analytics Data**: Anonymized data about how you interact with the app, such as features used or time spent, collected via third-party tools like Google Analytics.
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for crash reporting and improving app performance.

### Automatically Collected Data
- **IP Address**: Collected when you access the app to ensure secure connections.
- **Cookies and Trackers**: Used for analytics and personalized ads (if applicable).

If the app does not collect personal information, we explicitly state this in relevant sections to ensure transparency.

## How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data for the following purposes:
- **App Functionality**: To enable core features like video playback, editing, or sharing.
- **Improvement**: To analyze usage patterns and enhance app performance.
- **Personalization**: To provide tailored content or ads based on your preferences (if enabled).
- **Customer Support**: To respond to inquiries or troubleshoot issues.
- **Legal Compliance**: To comply with applicable laws or respond to legal requests.

## Sharing Your Information
We may share your data under the following circumstances:
- **Third-Party Services**:
  - **Google Analytics**: For usage analytics (see their privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  - **Ad Networks**: For personalized ads, if enabled (subject to your consent).
- **Service Providers**: Trusted partners who process data on our behalf (e.g., cloud storage providers) under strict confidentiality agreements.
- **Legal Obligations**: We may disclose data to comply with legal requirements, such as responding to law enforcement requests or protecting our rights.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## Data Storage and Security
- **Storage**: Data is stored on secure servers with encryption and access controls.
- **Retention**: We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined or as required by law.
- **Security Measures**: We use industry-standard measures like encryption and firewalls to protect your data from unauthorized access or breaches.

## Your Rights
Under applicable data privacy laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), you have the following rights:
- **Access**: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
- **Correction**: Request corrections to inaccurate data.
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations).
- **Opt-Out**: Opt out of data collection for analytics or ads via app settings.
- **Data Portability**: Request your data in a structured format.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.
